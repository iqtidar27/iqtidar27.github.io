Hello! 👋 I am AKM Iqtidar Newaz, seeking a PhD position in web security & privacy domain.

Nowadays, I am specifically exploring browser extensions' security and privacy, where my research interests include:
  • Understanding the execution environment and behavior of the browser extensions through static and dynamic analysis.
  • Vetting the browser extensions' privacy policy page against browser-specific user data policy guidelines.

Currently, I am working as a Lecturer at North South University

I completed my Master’s in Cybersecurity at Florida International University (FIU), where my thesis focused on End-to-end Security Analysis of Smart Healthcare Devices, Applications, and Communications. This research work presented novel security frameworks, adversarial ML-attacks for SHSs, and a systematic threat analysis approach for smart healthcare communication systems. These mechanisms aimed to cover three fundamental contributions: (i) An ML-based security framework for connected smart healthcare systems (SHSs), (ii) Adversarial machine learning (ML) attacks to exploit the ML classifiers used in a SHS, (iii) A systematic threat analysis for smart healthcare communication systems as well as intrusion detection system (IDS) to monitor and detect attacks on the network traffic of smart healthcare devices.
